# Citizen Complaint Management System - Overview

## Project Overview (Administration Pitch)

This system is an AI-driven, human-in-the-loop platform designed to streamline municipal complaint management.

* **Direct Citizen Engagement:** Citizens can easily submit complaints (with optional photos) through a simple form, making the process transparent and accessible.
* **Citizen Tracking:** Citizens can follow the status of their complaint at any time in the portal using their Ticket ID.
* **Smart Prioritization:** The system automatically identifies urgent issues, ensuring critical problems are addressed first.
* **Policy-Aligned Recommendations:** Every case comes with guidance based on official laws and regulations (maintained by the development team in the knowledge base), helping authorities make decisions that are compliant and fair.
* **Centralized Dashboard:** Authorities receive all complaints in one place, with clear status tracking from "Pending" to "Done" (Pending → Approved → In Progress → Verifying → Done) and in-app notifications for dashboard users.
* **Human Oversight:** Operators remain in control. The AI supports them with insights, but final decisions stay with the administration.
* **Accountability & Proof of Work:** Maintenance teams upload evidence of completed tasks, tickets move to "Verifying", and admins confirm before closing tickets, ensuring transparency.
* **Efficiency & Trust:** Citizens see faster responses, authorities save time, and the whole process builds public confidence in government services.
